PART ONE: THE ASCENT

The old road narrows here, and the ash comes down heavy enough that you tie the
red cord between you without discussing it. Somewhere above and to the left,
something is moving at the pace of a walking person, and it has been keeping
that pace for the better part of an hour.

The party begins at the foot of the old road with heavy ashfall already
underway. Any Wits roll depending on sight is one Difficulty harder for as long
as the fall lasts, and the Warden should say so out loud the first time it
matters rather than applying it silently.

PART TWO: WHAT FOLLOWS

Two cinder hounds have been tracking the party since the second milestone. They
will not attack in the open while the party is roped together, because the line
reads as one large warm thing rather than as several small ones. They wait for
the cord to come off.

APPENDIX: CONDITIONS

Held. A Held creature may not move away from whatever holds it, and rolls at a
penalty of two on every action that is not an attempt to escape.

Shaken. A Shaken creature rolls at a penalty of one until it takes a turn doing
nothing else.
